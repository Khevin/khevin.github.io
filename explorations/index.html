A starting recommendation

Signal’s conviction.
After Hours’ instinct.

Signal is the strongest starting point for your brief: immediate, memorable and clear about the work. After Hours is the expressive alternative if you want taste and personality to lead. Studio is the quietest, most systematic direction.

All three retain your introduction, principles, disciplines, clients, case studies, smaller work, process and contact. The case studies gain visible imagery, and “work” now takes readers straight to the projects.
